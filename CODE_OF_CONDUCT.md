# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Forgeline to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality, religion or appearance.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review.
- Assume good intent and welcome beginners.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for learners and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam, plagiarism or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they judge harmful.

Report problems to **codejborg@junaidul.pro.bd**. Reports are handled privately.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
